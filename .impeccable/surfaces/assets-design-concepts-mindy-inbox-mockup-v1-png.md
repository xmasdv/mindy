## Scope and mode
Primary Windows desktop inbox; Operate mode.

## Audience and job
Independent professionals process mail across multiple accounts without losing account, folder, or sender-identity context.

## Direction
Precision Workspace: deep-aubergine global rail, warm tonal panes, dense stable message rows, spacious reading, persistent search, restrained Mindy Violet state cues. The selected message is the focal moment; local assistance stays subordinate.

## Memorable moment
Moving from Unified Inbox into a message keeps both account origin and sending identity visibly anchored while the reading pane gains space.

## Constraints
The approved mockup is visual authority. Preserve inherited mail/security meaning but expose no Thunderbird visual chrome on the primary surface. Keyboard, 200% text, High Contrast, offline, empty, loading, error, and identity-ambiguity states are required.

Final logo asset and production validation of exact typography metrics.
